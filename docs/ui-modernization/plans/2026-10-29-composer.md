# Composer Implementation Plan (C12)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make the message composer a floating rounded pill inset from the conversation panel's edges, in both of Telegram's composer implementations, keeping every composer state: reply, edit, forward, link preview, autocomplete, voice and video recording, send-as, slowmode, scheduled, silent, and drag-and-drop.

**Architecture:** `ComposeControls` already paints a rounded background when its style has `radius > 0` (`ComposeControls::paintBackground`, used by the stories composer). The sections that build it pass a Soft Panels style with radius 18 and a transparent field background, and place it with outer margins. `HistoryWidget`, which has its own composer, paints the same pill in `drawField` and moves its controls inward by the same margins. Both paint the Composer role, so the pill is denser than the canvas while material is active.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, Qt 6.11 Widgets, `chat_helpers/chat_helpers.style`, `HistoryView::ComposeControls`, `HistoryWidget`.

**Spec:** [architecture.md, Element styling](../architecture.md#element-styling-c9c15); exploration CSS `.composer-wrap` (padding 0 16 14), `.composer` (height 50, radius 18, 0.5 px edge, shadow 0 3 12 at 8 %), `.send` (36 px round, accent fill when sending, plain when recording).

**Visual reference:** [C12 composer](../component-references/c12-composer/README.md): the mockup's composer, every state inside the pill, and the geometry against today's full-width area.

**Depends on:** [shell layout](2026-10-29-shell-layout.md), [surface roles](2026-10-29-surface-roles.md) (C4 converted both composer fills to the Composer role).

## Global Constraints

- All [ground rules](README.md#ground-rules).
- At 100 % scale: outer margins `16, 0, 16, 14` (left, top, right, bottom), radius `18`, border `Border(Surface::Composer)`. The exploration's drop shadow is not reproduced; the border carries the edge.
- Field, buttons and their sizes stay Telegram's (`historySendSize` 44 × 46, `historyComposeField`); the pill grows with the field's height and with reply, edit, forward and preview strips, which sit inside the pill.
- The send button keeps Telegram's state machine (send, record voice, record video, slowmode, schedule, edit save); this plan restyles none of it.
- The stories and video-comment composers (`storiesComposeControls`) are unchanged.

## Review Focus

- A long multi-line message (field grows to its maximum height): the pill grows upward and the message list's bottom stays above the pill. Pinned by Task 1 Step 4 and Task 2 Step 4.
- Voice recording (hold the record button): the record bar's background must take the pill's shape, not a square across the panel. Pinned by Task 3 Step 2.
- Emoji, sticker and GIF autocomplete and the bot command list: their popups anchor above the pill, not above the old full-width area. Pinned by Task 1 Step 4.
- Write-restricted chats (channels without rights, banned users): the restriction label sits inside the pill. Pinned by Task 1 Step 4.
- Bot keyboard shown in a bot chat: it sits inside the pill under the field row, not as a full-width block below it. Pinned by Task 2 Step 4.
- Dragging files over the chat: the drag area overlays the whole conversation panel as today. Pinned by Task 2 Step 4.

### Task 1: Rounded `ComposeControls`

**Files:**
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/chat_helpers/chat_helpers.style`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/history/view/controls/history_view_compose_controls.cpp`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/history/view/history_view_chat_section.cpp`, `history_view_scheduled_section.cpp`, `history_view_welcome_messages_section.cpp`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/settings/business/settings_shortcut_messages.cpp`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces style objects in `chat_helpers.style`: `softPanelsComposeControls: ComposeControls(defaultComposeControls)` with `radius: 18px` and `field: InputField(historyComposeField) { textBg: transparent; }`; `softPanelsComposeMargins: margins(16px, 0px, 16px, 14px)`.
- Produces, file-local in each section: placement of `_composeControls` inside the section rect shrunk by `softPanelsComposeMargins` when `Ui::Material::SoftPanels()`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Pass the style**

Each listed section passes `&st::softPanelsComposeControls` as the composer's style when `Ui::Material::SoftPanels()`; the constructor already turns `radius > 0` into `_backgroundRect`.

- [ ] **Step 2: Paint the pill through the Composer role**

In `ComposeControls::paintBackground`, when `_backgroundRect` is set and `Ui::Material::Active()`, paint the rounded rects with `Ui::Material::PaintSurface(p, path, Surface::Composer, _st.bg)` instead of `drawRoundedRect` with `_st.bg`; stroke the border when `SoftPanels()`. The `FieldHeader` fill (`st::historyComposeAreaBg` in its paint request) and the rich draft preview fills (`history_view_rich_draft_preview.cpp`) paint nothing under Soft Panels, because the pill is behind them.

- [ ] **Step 3: Place the composer with margins**

In each section's geometry function, shrink the composer's width by the horizontal margins, move it right by the left margin and up by the bottom margin, and end the message list above the pill's top. Keep the existing bottom-anchored behavior otherwise.

Pill encloses every strip; popups anchor above it.

- Produces, in `HistoryWidget`: `[[nodiscard]] QRect composerPillRect() const;` — the area today's `drawField` fills, shrunk horizontally and lifted by the margins under Soft Panels, and including the bot keyboard while it is shown; the same rect otherwise.

- [ ] **Step 1: Field style**

Construct `_field` with the Task 1 field style when `Ui::Material::SoftPanels()`.

- [ ] **Step 2: Paint**

In `HistoryWidget::drawField`, paint `composerPillRect()` as a rounded rect with the Composer role and border under Soft Panels; otherwise keep the square `st::historyReplyBg` fill. `BotKeyboard::paintEvent` fills its area with `st::historyComposeAreaBg`; under Soft Panels it paints nothing, because the pill is behind it.

In `updateControlsGeometry`, place `_kbScroll` inside the pill by the same margins, so the bot keyboard sits in the pill under the field row instead of across the full width, and end the scroll area above the pill.

### Task 3: Recording and send button

**Files:**
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/chat_helpers/chat_helpers.style`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/history/view/controls/history_view_voice_record_bar.cpp`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/history/history_widget.cpp`
- Check: `Telegram/SourceFiles/ui/controls/send_button.cpp`

- [ ] **Step 1: Send button schedule glyph**

`SendButton` paints no background of its own. `st::historyComposeAreaBg` appears only in the schedule state: as the `historyScheduleIcon` glyph color over the accent circle, and as the ripple tint at alpha 25 in `SendButton::paintSchedule`. Under material, check the schedule state over the Composer fill; recolor the glyph only if it reads as a different tint from the pill.

- [ ] **Step 2: Record bar background**

`RecordBar` already has a `radius`: `VoiceRecordBar::init()` turns `radius > 0` into a rounded `_backgroundRect`. Add `record: RecordBar(defaultRecordBar) { radius: 18px; }` to `softPanelsComposeControls`; `ComposeControls` already passes its style's `record` through `VoiceRecordBarDescriptor::stOverride`. `HistoryWidget` builds its record bar with the constructor that takes no descriptor, which uses the default style, so switch it to the descriptor form with that `stOverride` under Soft Panels. While material is active, paint the rounded background through `Ui::Material::PaintSurface(..., Surface::Composer, _st.bg)`. The bar spans the composer's width at the bottom, so with a strip above it its top corners round inside the pill; check that, the cancel-by-swipe animation and the locked recording state.
