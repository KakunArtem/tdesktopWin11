# Messages Implementation Plan (C13)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Keep messages readable and coherent on the Soft Panels canvas: service messages, date pills, the unread bar and media captions adopt the material when the "Window material" chat background is on, and bubbles, reactions and attachments are verified in every mirrored chat section.

**Architecture:** Telegram's bubbles are already close to the exploration (opaque fills, `BubbleRadiusLarge()` 16 px against the exploration's 14 px, small radius on grouped corners), so bubble geometry is not changed. What changes is the chat style's service and unread-bar colors, which Telegram derives from the wallpaper's average color: while the material chat background is active, `SessionController` overrides them with the Bar fill and readable text through `Ui::ChatStyle`, the same way the background preview already overrides the service color.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, Qt 6.11, `Ui::ChatStyle` / `Ui::ChatTheme` (`ui/chat/`, `td_ui`), `Window::SessionController`.

**Spec:** [architecture.md, Element styling](../architecture.md#element-styling-c9c15); exploration CSS `.bubble` (radius 14 / 5, 96 % fill), `.date` (radius 12, 80 % surface, secondary text), `.reaction`, `.meta`; the exploration's chat wallpaper note.

**Depends on:** [conversation chrome](2026-10-29-conversation-chrome.md) (`Window::Material::ChatBackgroundActive()`, `ChatBackgroundActiveValue()`), [material fills](2026-10-29-material-fills.md).

## Global Constraints

- All [ground rules](README.md#ground-rules).
- Bubble fills stay opaque palette colors (`msgInBg`, `msgOutBg` and the chat theme's), so message text keeps ClearType and its current contrast.
- Bubble radii stay `BubbleRadiusLarge()` / `BubbleRadiusSmall()` and the existing small-radius option; changing them is a user decision.
- With the material chat background off, every color here is exactly Telegram's (wallpaper-derived).
- Service and unread-bar overrides while the material background is active: background `Fill(Surface::Bar)`; text `windowFg`, raised by `Ui::EnsureTextContrast` to 4.5:1 over that fill composited on black and white.
- All mirrored sections are covered: `HistoryWidget`, chat section (topics, replies, saved sublists), pinned, scheduled, admin log, welcome messages.

## Review Focus

- A chat theme from the other side (custom bubble gradient) with the material background on: outgoing gradient bubbles keep their theme and readability floors; only service colors change. Pinned by Task 1 Step 4.
- Stickers, round videos and single photos without a bubble: their time and views pills use the service background and must stay readable on the material. Pinned by Task 2 Step 2.
- Selecting messages (selection overlay `msgSelectOverlay`): selected service messages use the "selected" service colors, which the override must also set. Pinned by Task 1 Step 2.
- Night mode switch with the material background on: overrides are recomputed for the new palette. Pinned by Task 1 Step 4.
- Videos and round videos playing inline: drawn correctly over the material canvas with no black rectangle. Pinned by Task 2 Step 3.

### Task 1: Service colors on the material background

**Files:**
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/ui/chat/chat_style.h`, `chat_style.cpp`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/window/window_session_controller.h`, `window_session_controller.cpp`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `ChatStyle::applyAdjustedServiceBg(QColor)` (exists), `Ui::EnsureTextContrast` (C3), `Ui::Material::Fill(Surface::Bar)`, `Window::Material::ChatBackgroundActiveValue()` (C11).
- Produces, in `Ui::ChatStyle`:
  - `void applyAdjustedServiceFg(QColor serviceFg);` — mirrors `applyAdjustedServiceBg` for the service text color and its dependent icon colors.
  - `void applyAdjustedServiceSelected(QColor bg, QColor fg);` — the `msgServiceBgSelected` / `msgServiceFgSelected` pair (recheck names in `ChatStyle`).
  - `void applyAdjustedUnreadBar(QColor bg, QColor border);` — for `historyUnreadBarBg` / `historyUnreadBarBorder`.
- Produces, in `Window::SessionController`: `void applyMaterialChatColors();` — re-applies the chat style theme, then the overrides when `ChatBackgroundActive()`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Add the three `ChatStyle` setters**

Each sets the color and rebuilds the corner caches that depend on it, the way `applyAdjustedServiceBg`'s callers expect (the corner caches for `msgServiceBg` are reset in the code that assigns `msgServiceBgCornersSmall` / `Large`).

- [ ] **Step 2: Apply overrides in `SessionController`**

At the end of `setChatStyleTheme`, and on every `ChatBackgroundActiveValue()`, `Ui::Material::FillsChanged()` and `style::PaletteChanged()` emission, call `applyMaterialChatColors()`. When active: service bg `Fill(Bar)`; service fg readable `windowFg`; selected service bg the Bar fill blended 15 % toward `windowBgActive` (recheck), selected fg readable over it; unread bar bg `Fill(Bar)`, border `Border(Bar)`. When not active it only re-applies the theme, restoring Telegram's derived colors.

- [ ] **Step 3: Build**

Run: `cmd /c .ai\setup\build-target.cmd Telegram`

- [ ] **Step 4: Check**

With material and the material background on, in a group with joins and pinned-message service messages: service messages, date pills and the floating date are Bar pills with dark text (light theme) or light text (dark theme); select them; scroll to show the unread bar. Switch night mode. Open a chat with a custom theme: outgoing bubbles unchanged. Turn the material background off: Telegram's wallpaper-derived colors return.
