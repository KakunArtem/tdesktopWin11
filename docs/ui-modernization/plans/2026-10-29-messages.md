# Messages Implementation Plan (C13)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Keep messages readable and coherent on the Soft Panels canvas: service messages, date pills, the unread bar and media captions adopt the material when the "Window material" chat background is on, and bubbles, reactions and attachments are verified in every mirrored chat section.

**Architecture:** Telegram's bubbles are already close to the exploration (opaque fills, `BubbleRadiusLarge()` 16 px against the exploration's 14 px, small radius on grouped corners), so bubble geometry changes only in its large radius: 14 px under Soft Panels, the exploration's value (user decision 2026-10-31). What changes is the chat style's service and unread-bar colors. `ChatTheme` derives `msgServiceBg`, `msgServiceBgSelected` and the history scroll bar colors from the wallpaper's average color; `msgServiceFg` (`windowFgActive`, white) is a fixed palette color chosen for that dark, wallpaper-tinted pill, and the unread bar is a fixed opaque palette strip. While the material chat background is active, `SessionController` overrides them with the Bar fill and readable text through `Ui::ChatStyle`, the same way the background preview already overrides the service color.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, Qt 6.11, `Ui::ChatStyle` / `Ui::ChatTheme` (`ui/chat/`, `td_ui`), `Window::SessionController`.

**Spec:** [architecture.md, Element styling](../architecture.md#element-styling-c9c15); exploration CSS `.bubble` (radius 14 / 5, 96 % fill), `.date` (radius 12, 80 % surface, secondary text), `.reaction`, `.meta`; the exploration's chat wallpaper note.

**Visual reference:** [C13 messages](../component-references/c13-messages/README.md): the mockup's messages, service elements on a wallpaper and on the material background in day and night, and their contrast.

**Depends on:** [conversation chrome](2026-10-29-conversation-chrome.md) (`Window::Material::ChatBackgroundActive()`, `ChatBackgroundActiveValue()`), [material fills](2026-10-29-material-fills.md).

## Global Constraints

- All [ground rules](README.md#ground-rules).
- Bubble fills stay opaque palette colors (`msgInBg`, `msgOutBg` and the chat theme's), so message text keeps ClearType and its current contrast.
- Large bubble radius under Soft Panels: `bubbleRadiusSoftPanels` 14 px (user decision 2026-10-31). `BubbleRadiusSmall()`, grouped corners and the "Use small message bubble radius" option stay; with that option on, it wins.
- With the material chat background off, every color here is exactly Telegram's (wallpaper-derived).
- Service and unread-bar overrides while the material background is active: background `Fill(Surface::Bar)`; service text `windowFg` and unread bar text `historyUnreadBarFg`, each raised by `Ui::EnsureTextContrast` to 4.5:1 over that fill composited on black and white.
- All mirrored sections are covered: `HistoryWidget`, chat section (topics, replies, saved sublists), pinned, scheduled, admin log, welcome messages.

## Review Focus

- A chat theme from the other side (custom bubble gradient) with the material background on: outgoing gradient bubbles keep their theme and readability floors; only service colors change. Pinned by Task 1 Step 5.
- Stickers, round videos and single photos without a bubble: their time and views pills use the service background and must stay readable on the material. Pinned by Task 2 Step 2.
- Selecting messages (selection overlay `msgSelectOverlay`): selected service messages use the "selected" service colors, which the override must also set. Pinned by Task 1 Step 3.
- Night mode switch with the material background on: overrides are recomputed for the new palette. Pinned by Task 1 Step 5.
- Videos and round videos playing inline: drawn correctly over the material canvas with no black rectangle. Pinned by Task 2 Step 3.

### Task 1: Service colors on the material background

**Files:**
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/ui/chat/chat_style.h`, `chat_style.cpp`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/ui/chat/chat_style_radius.cpp`, `ui/chat/chat.style`
- Modify: `Telegram/SourceFiles/window/window_session_controller.h`, `window_session_controller.cpp`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `ChatStyle::applyAdjustedServiceBg(QColor)` (exists), `Ui::EnsureTextContrast` (C3), `Ui::Material::Fill(Surface::Bar)`, `Window::Material::ChatBackgroundActiveValue()` (C11).
- Produces, in `Ui::ChatStyle`:
  - `void applyAdjustedServiceFg(QColor serviceFg);` — mirrors `applyAdjustedServiceBg` for the service text color and its dependent icon colors.
  - `void applyAdjustedServiceSelected(QColor bg);` — `msgServiceBgSelected`. There is no selected service text color: selected service messages keep `msgServiceFg`.
  - `void applyAdjustedUnreadBar(QColor bg, QColor border, QColor fg);` — for `historyUnreadBarBg`, `historyUnreadBarBorder` and `historyUnreadBarFg`.
  - `void applyScrollColors(not_null<const style::palette*> palette);` — copies `historyScrollBg`, `historyScrollBgOver`, `historyScrollBarBg` and `historyScrollBarBgOver` from `palette`.
- Produces, in `Window::SessionController`: `void applyMaterialChatColors();` — re-applies the chat style theme with `_chatStyle->apply()` (not `setChatStyleTheme`, which returns early for the same theme), then the overrides when `ChatBackgroundActive()`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Bubble radius**

Add `bubbleRadiusSoftPanels: 14px` next to `bubbleRadiusLarge` in `chat.style`. `BubbleRadiusLarge()` computes a function-local static on first call: return `st::bubbleRadiusSmall` when the small-radius option is on, else `st::bubbleRadiusSoftPanels` under `Ui::Material::SoftPanels()`, else `st::bubbleRadiusLarge`. `SoftPanels()` is set at startup before any bubble is painted; recheck that order. `MsgFileThumbRadiusLarge()` keeps its value. Check an album, a reply, a sticker with a caption and the small-radius option.

- [ ] **Step 2: Add the four `ChatStyle` setters**

Each sets its colors, then resets the caches `ChatStyle::assignPalette` resets for them: `_serviceBgCornersNormal`, `_serviceBgCornersInverted` and every image style's `msgServiceBgCornersSmall` / `msgServiceBgCornersLarge`. Then it increments the palette version and fires `paletteChanged()` so open views repaint. The existing `applyAdjustedServiceBg` does none of this: the background preview box calls it before anything is painted. Calls made while a chat is open need it, so the override path resets them once after all setters.

- [ ] **Step 3: Apply overrides in `SessionController`**

At the end of `setChatStyleTheme`, and on every `ChatBackgroundActiveValue()`, `Ui::Material::FillsChanged()` and `style::PaletteChanged()` emission, call `applyMaterialChatColors()`. When active: service bg `Fill(Bar)`; service fg readable `windowFg`, checked over both the plain and the selected fill; selected service bg the Bar fill blended 15 % toward `windowBgActive`; unread bar bg `Fill(Bar)`, border `Border(Bar)`, fg readable `historyUnreadBarFg`; `applyScrollColors(style::main_palette::get())`, since the wallpaper they were tinted for is hidden. When not active it only re-applies the theme, restoring Telegram's derived colors.

- [ ] **Step 4: Build**

Run: `cmd /c .ai\setup\build-target.cmd Telegram`

- [ ] **Step 5: Check**

With material and the material background on, in a group with joins and pinned-message service messages: service messages, date pills and the floating date are Bar pills with dark text (light theme) or light text (dark theme); select them; scroll to show the unread bar. Switch night mode. Open a chat with a custom theme: outgoing bubbles unchanged. Turn the material background off: Telegram's wallpaper-derived colors return.
