C3 Material fills: visual reference

Fills computed with the plan's algorithm (compute-fills.py) and drawn over the worst-case backdrops and the Blue folds wallpaper. Orange alphas were raised above the role floor to keep text readable.

A. Role fills for the mockup palette

Text #132335 / #f2f6fb and secondary #3a4b5f / #c2cfdd on surface #e9eef5 / #1b2838. "Worst" is the lowest text or secondary contrast over black and white at the chosen alpha.

B. Telegram's own themes: secondary text adjusted, then fills

Default day and night secondary text cannot reach 4.5:1 through any translucent fill, so without adjustment every fill is opaque. ReadableOver darkens or lightens it in OKLab, then the fills drop to their floors.

C. Border: primary text at 10%

A 1 px edge between the shell and a panel, magnified 12 times. BorderColor(text) keeps the text's color and uses alpha 0.10, as the mockup's --edge.

D. Material inactive

When Active() is false, Fill() returns the opaque role color and every adjusted entry is written back exactly.
