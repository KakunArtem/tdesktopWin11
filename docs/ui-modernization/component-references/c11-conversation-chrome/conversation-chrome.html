C11 Conversation chrome: visual reference

The header becomes part of the conversation panel, bars become inset pills, and an option shows the window material behind messages instead of the wallpaper.

A. Target: header and pinned pill from the mockup at 100%

No shadow under the header: its bottom edge is the panel's continuous fill. The pinned message is an inset pill with the accent line of MessageBar.

B. Several bars at once: pills stack with a 4 px gap

Each bar keeps its widget, animation, close button and click target; PaintBar draws the Conversation role across the row and a Bar-role pill inside margins 12, 0, 12, 4 with radius 9. The group call, requests and translate pills are proposals: the mockup only has the pinned one.

C. "Show window material behind messages"

A new checkbox in Chat settings, after "Adaptive layout for wide screens", shown only when the window can use material. Off: the user's wallpaper fills the message area, as today. On, with material active: the Conversation role instead.
