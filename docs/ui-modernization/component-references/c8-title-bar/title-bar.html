C8 Title bar: visual reference

The custom title area painted with the Window role, with Telegram's caption buttons and window behavior unchanged. Material values are C3's for the night theme (Window 0.81); title colors are from night.tdesktop-theme.

A. Target: the mockup's 32 px title bar at 125% zoom

No background of its own: the window material continues into the title. The brand icon and "Telegram" text are mockup decoration; on Windows, Telegram draws title text only in canary builds.

B. Night theme states: the caption glyphs are what must stay readable

Glyph contrast against the title background, icon target 3:1. Today's glyph #576673 already measures 2.29:1 on the opaque title and falls to 1.50:1 over the material on a white backdrop; adjusted to #9eaebc it keeps 3:1 over black and white.

C. Caption buttons keep their hover colors and snap layouts

Hover backgrounds are Telegram's opaque colors over the material; the close button keeps its red hover. Hovering maximize for a second still opens the Windows 11 snap layouts flyout.
