C7 Shell layout: visual reference

The Soft Panels shell: the navigation panel flush with the left edge, a rounded conversation panel and an optional third column, separated by gaps over the window material. Sizes are at 100% interface scale.

A. Target: the readable mockup at 100%

Measured marks on the mockup. Its title bar (32 px) belongs to C8; its rail and list styling to C9 and C10.

B. Panel rectangles from Layout() in the three adaptive layouts

The plan's test vectors, in MainWidget coordinates. The folder rail sits left of MainWidget, the frame inset (right 6, bottom 6) outside it. Column widths and thresholds stay Telegram's.

C. Style values and the panel frame corner

PanelFrame paints the area outside the rounded shape with the Window role, then strokes the shape. Magnified 8 times: a conversation corner (Corners::All) and the navigation panel's top-right corner (Corners::Right).
