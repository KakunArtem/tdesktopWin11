C17 Shared controls: visual reference

Under Soft Panels a control skips a fill in the surface color and turns the opaque windowBgOver hover into the translucent hover the chats list uses. Filled buttons, marks, borders, ripples and text keep their look; three state colors are raised on the material.

A. Mockup targets: a switch and a field with no fill of their own

The readable mockup's main menu switch keeps a filled knob on its track, and the composer field has no background: the pill behind it is the only fill. Crops of the C9 and C7 captures at 100%.

B. On a panel: Chat settings in the Conversation role

Today every settings row, field and light button paints windowBg under itself, and hover paints windowBgOver: opaque patches over the 0.79 / 0.81 fill. With soft controls they rest on the panel, hover is windowFg at 6 % / 7 %, and the divider band takes the same tint.

C. In a box: Add Members in the Popup role

Boxes fill 0.97, so patches are faint there; the hover, the multi-select field and the list rows change the most. The selection ring around the avatar check stays.

D. Colors and the mapping

Contrast is the worst case over the strictest role floor (Navigation 0.78 in day, Conversation 0.81 in night) on a black and a white backdrop, and on the opaque surface for solid mode.
