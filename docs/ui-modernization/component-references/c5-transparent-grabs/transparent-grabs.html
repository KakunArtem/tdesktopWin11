C5 Transparent grabs: visual reference

Frames of a section change (a chat sliding to its info) and of a cross-fade, over the Blue folds wallpaper with the day theme's Conversation fill (0.79). The density label is the fill alpha behind the content at the frame's center.

A. Slide: content-only caches over one background

Ui::Material::Grab captures content with a transparent background; the owner calls PaintAnimationBackground once, then paints the caches. The old section is clipped where the new one covers it and dimmed by slideFadeOutBg, as today. The other rows are the failures the plan's recordings look for.

B. Cross-fade: constant density at mid-animation

Window::SlideAnimation with fade overlaps the departing and arriving caches while both are partly transparent. Shown here in place, as the overlap area: with fills inside the caches their combined alpha dips at the midpoint and the backdrop brightens through.
