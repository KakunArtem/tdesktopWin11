C14 Transient surfaces: visual reference

Menus, panels, autocomplete, the reaction selector, boxes and toasts share one elevated look: radius 12, menu highlights with radius 8 inset by 4 px, Border(Popup), and the Popup fill (0.97) for in-app layers. Popup windows stay opaque until the Acrylic prototype's go/no-go.

A. Target: the mockup's details popup and main menu at 100%

The details popup: radius 13, the surface at 97 %, a thin edge and a soft shadow. The main menu's rows: rounded highlights with radius 8. Telegram's context menus have no mockup design; they follow these two.

B. Surfaces in the day theme, over the conversation material

Telegram's day colors; the hover color is approximate. Each surface keeps its content, keyboard navigation, dismissal and positioning.

C. Surfaces in the night theme

Colors from night.tdesktop-theme: menuBg and boxBg #17212b, windowBgOver #232e3c for highlights, tooltipBg #16222d, toastBg #000000b2, layerBg #0000007f.

D. Shapes and where each value lives

Popup windows (top-level, lib_ui) and in-app layers (children of the main window, styled by the app) are changed in different places.
