C12 Composer: visual reference

The composer becomes a rounded pill inset from the conversation panel's edges, in both composer implementations, keeping every state. Field, buttons and their sizes stay Telegram's.

A. Target: the mockup's composer at 100%

Margins 16, 0, 16, 14; radius 18; the Composer role (0.96) with a thin edge. The mockup is 50 px high with 36 px buttons; Telegram's row stays 46 px with its own buttons. The mockup's drop shadow is not reproduced.

B. States: every strip and bar sits inside the pill

Strips take Telegram's heights (reply, edit, forward and preview 49 px) and the pill grows upward with them and with the field, up to its maximum height. Popups anchor above the pill. Orange tags mark proposals the plan leaves open.

C. Geometry: today's square area and the Soft Panels pill

Both composers keep their controls and hit areas; the pill is the same area shrunk by the margins, with the message list ending above it.
