C15 Info, settings and specialized screens: visual reference

The info pane and settings sit on the panel that hosts them; lock and sign-in screens put their content on a card over the window material. Media viewer, calls, Mini Apps and notification windows stay opaque. There is no mockup design for these screens; they follow the shell, C11 and C14.

A. Info pane: in the third column and as a layer

ContentWidget fills profileBg in a column and boxBg in a layer. Under Soft Panels: the Conversation role in the third panel, the Popup role in the layer's rounded box.

B. Settings rows: the content fill alone is not enough

Every SettingsButton row paints its own textBg (windowBg) today. If only ContentWidget changes, the rows stay as opaque strips over the material. The rows' resting fill has to go, keeping the hover and ripple.

C. Lock and sign-in: Window role on the body, a Popup card behind the content

Over the plan's test wallpapers, Night and Bright sky. The passcode header and error line are painted by PasscodeLockWidget itself, so the card encloses them. The QR code keeps its white square.

D. Promotion: from an experimental option to a setting

Task 3 replaces the soft-panels-layout option with the KV pref soft_panels_layout, next to the transparency checkbox, with C2's restart confirmation. An existing option value is carried over on first read. The defaults are the user's decision.
